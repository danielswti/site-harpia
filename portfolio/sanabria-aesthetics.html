Sanabria Aesthetics is a national leader in no-shave hair transplant, delivering visible results in just 15 hours — a benchmark in medical precision, natural outcomes, and fast recovery.

We developed the Sanabria Aesthetics website to highlight its key differentiator: no-shave hair transplant with 15-hour visible results.

The digital experience was crafted to be clear, modern, and conversion-driven — reinforcing medical authority, showcasing advanced techniques, and presenting real outcomes.

The final platform communicates expertise, trust, and innovation, positioning the clinic as a leading hair restoration reference in both Brazil and the United States.
