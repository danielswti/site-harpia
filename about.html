We design strategies that move markets.

For more than two decades working across branding, design, and visual identity, we have partnered with founders, companies, and high-growth organizations shaping today’s business landscape.

Throughout this journey, one truth became clear: many organizations carry extraordinary potential — yet few succeed in translating that value into a brand the market can truly see, feel, and believe in.

In the current environment, brands do not compete only for attention — they compete for meaning, coherence, and relevance. These elements determine who leads, who grows, and who fades into the background.

This is why we developed our proprietary methodology. We don’t simply create brands — we refine, elevate, and engineer them. We unite identity, positioning, symbolism, and narrative into one coherent system capable of transforming brands into objects of desire, influence, and long-term value.

If your business is ready to move beyond mere presence and into authority, distinction, and lasting relevance, this is where your next evolution begins.

ONE METHOD. THREE DIMENSIONS.

FOUNDER Led-Growth

Growth begins with the founder. We build personal brands that align vision, voice, and presence — turning the founder into the main source of clarity, influence, and credibility inside the business.

  1. Personal brand strategy and image assessment
  2. Strategic founder positioning
  3. Voice, tone, and narrative system
  4. Signature visual system (logos, colors, typography)
  5. Launch / relaunch playbook and content focus

COMPANY Branding

A strong company brand aligns culture, identity, and direction. We create systems that elevate perception, strengthen authority, and prepare the business to scale with discipline.

  1. Brand value proposition and purpose
  2. Positioning, naming, and architecture
  3. Core narrative and messaging system
  4. Visual identity system and applications
  5. Internal brand activation and governance

PRODUCT Branding

Products become powerful when identity meets aspiration. We design product brands that communicate value instantly and reinforce the wider ecosystem around founder and company.

  1. Naming and diagnostic
  2. Positioning and value proposition
  3. Packaging & visual system
  4. Launch strategy and key visuals
  5. Communication and retail / digital guidelines

ORIGIN

Harpia was born from a story of VISION and RESILIENCE.

From early sketches on bedroom walls to boardrooms across continents, Harpia emerged from a founder who refused to accept ordinary outcomes. Every setback became material for a stronger vision, every limitation a new frontier to cross.

That same resilience now guides how we build brands: with discipline, clarity, and the courage to challenge what’s expected.

Its founder, Regis Duran, began painting at five and selling designs at twelve.

Over 23 years, he has built more than 2,500 brands — from early-stage ventures to multimillion-dollar empires.

The name Harpia

Inspired by the harpy eagle, the largest eagle in the Americas, Harpia embodies a way of building brands that combines precision in the hunt with patience in the flight.

More than a symbol, it is a discipline: to see further, to act with intention, and to protect what truly matters in a brand.

It stands for REBIRTH, DISCIPLINE, and POWER.
